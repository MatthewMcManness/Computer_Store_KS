API Integration Analysis for Custom PC Builder

This document analyzes which IT suppliers offer real-time API access for pricing integration and dropshipping capabilities to support the Phase 5 PC Builder feature.

⚠

NOT RECOMMENDED: Amazon Business for Reseller Sourcing

While retail arbitrage is legal under the First Sale Doctrine, Amazon Business has specific Terms of Service restrictions that make it unsuitable for reseller sourcing.

  • Business Prime Shipping Restriction: "Amazon Business customers are not permitted to use Business Prime shipping benefits to purchase products for the purpose of resale, rental, or to ship products to their customers or other parties." (Source)
  • No Third-Party Shipping: "Amazon Business customers are not permitted to use their Business Account to ship products to their customers or other third parties who are not linked to their organization."
  • No Dropship Capability: Amazon does not offer a dropship or fulfillment program for resellers
  • No Buyer API: There is no legitimate API to programmatically purchase from Amazon or retrieve real-time pricing for integration

Important Distinction: Retail arbitrage (buying products and reselling them) is legal under the US First Sale Doctrine. However, using Amazon Business Prime benefits for resale purchases violates Amazon's Terms of Service, which can result in account suspension. This is a breach of contract, not a criminal matter. For PC Builder integration, Amazon lacks the APIs, dropship programs, and reseller infrastructure that proper IT distributors provide.

4. Tier 2: Limited Suitability

Newegg Business FFR Program Closed

The Fulfillment for Reseller (FFR) dropship program is closed to new integrations. Their API is designed for sellers managing their Newegg marketplace presence, not for buyers placing orders.

What Works:

  • Seller API (if you sell ON Newegg as a marketplace)
  • PunchOut for enterprise procurement

What Doesn't Work:

  • ✗ No buyer API for purchasing
  • ✗ FFR dropship closed to new customers
  • ✗ Cannot place orders programmatically

Alternative: Could use as a sales channel (sell your builds ON Newegg) rather than as a supplier.

Dell Partner Direct Use Distributors

Dell APIs require formal partnership agreements and are not publicly accessible. For most resellers, accessing Dell products through Ingram Micro or TD Synnex is more practical.

Limitations:

  • No public product/pricing API
  • APIs only for Premier account partners
  • 100-unit minimum for refurbished bulk
  • No traditional dropship program

Recommendation: Access Dell products through Ingram Micro or TD Synnex distributor APIs instead of pursuing direct Dell partnership.

5. Not Recommended for PC Builder Integration

⚠ Amazon Business NOT SUITABLE

Amazon Business lacks the reseller infrastructure needed for PC Builder integration and has ToS restrictions on using Business Prime for resale.

Terms of Service Restrictions:

  • Business Prime Shipping: "Amazon Business customers are not permitted to use Business Prime shipping benefits to purchase products for the purpose of resale, rental, or to ship products to their customers or other parties." (Source)
  • No Third-Party Shipping: Cannot ship to customers or third parties not linked to your organization
  • No Dropship Program: Amazon does not offer dropship or fulfillment services for resellers

Legal Context:

  • First Sale Doctrine: Retail arbitrage (buying and reselling products) IS legal under US law
  • ToS vs. Law: Using Business Prime benefits for resale violates Amazon's Terms of Service, which is a breach of contract—not a criminal matter
  • Risk: Account suspension/termination, not legal prosecution

Why It's Not Suitable for PC Builder:

  • No buyer API for programmatic purchasing or real-time pricing
  • No reseller program or volume pricing structure
  • No dropship capability—you'd need to receive and reship all orders
  • Product Advertising API is for affiliates, not procurement
  • Third-party scraping tools violate Amazon ToS

💡 Bottom Line:

Amazon Business is designed for companies purchasing supplies for internal use, not for resellers. For PC Builder integration, use proper IT distributors (Ingram Micro, TD Synnex, D&H) which offer reseller APIs, volume pricing, and dropship programs specifically designed for this purpose.

Integration Strategy

  1. Primary Supplier (Ingram Micro): Best API documentation, official Node.js SDK, real-time pricing
  2. Secondary Supplier (TD Synnex): Inventory redundancy, branded dropshipping via MyShop
  3. Product Content (Etilize): Rich specifications, images, compatibility data

Rate Limit Considerations

  • Ingram Micro: 60 GET requests/minute/endpoint
  • TD Synnex: Varies by endpoint
  • D&H: Recommend every 2 hours, not every 15-30 minutes

Solution: Implement caching layer (Supabase) to reduce API calls. Cache pricing for 15-30 minutes, inventory for 5 minutes.

7. Implementation Roadmap

Phase 1: Account Setup (Week 1-2)

  • Apply for Ingram Micro reseller account at ingrammicroresellerapplication.com
  • Request API access via api@ingrammicro.com once approved
  • Apply for TD Synnex reseller account
  • Request API access via helpdeskus@tdsynnex.com
  • Set up MyShop for branded dropshipping
  • Gather required documentation (Tax ID, reseller cert, W-9)

Phase 2: Development (Week 3-4)

  • Install Ingram Micro Node.js SDK: npm install xi_sdk_resellers
  • Develop in sandbox environment
  • Build caching layer for pricing/inventory
  • Implement product search functionality
  • Create order placement workflow

Phase 3: Testing & Launch (Week 5-6)

  • Test with real products in sandbox
  • Verify dropship workflow end-to-end
  • Request production API credentials
  • Deploy PC Builder feature
