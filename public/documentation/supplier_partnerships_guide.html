1. Priority Summary & Quick Wins

Based on The Computer Store's business model (retail sales, repairs, custom builds, and MSP services), here are the recommended partnership priorities:

HIGH PRIORITY - Apply Immediately

  • D&H Distributing - Best for SMB-focused resellers, dedicated account reps, excellent training
  • iFixit Pro - 30% savings on repair parts, Microsoft/Google/Valve official parts
  • Ingram Micro - Largest selection (280,000+ products), essential for inventory depth
  • Lenovo Partner Program - Low barrier to entry, good margins on business laptops

MEDIUM PRIORITY - Apply Within 30 Days

  • ASUS Valued Partner Program - Essential for custom gaming builds (motherboards, GPUs)
  • MSI Partner Program - Gaming laptops and components
  • Dell Partner Program - Business systems, refurbished outlet access
  • HP Partner Program - Printers, business systems, consumer PCs

LONGER-TERM - Apply as Volume Increases

  • TD Synnex - Advanced solutions focus, better for larger MSPs
  • Intel Partner Alliance - Points/rewards system, useful at higher volumes
  • AMD Partner Network - Training and rebates, good for Ryzen builds
  • Refurbished Bulk Suppliers - Dell Refurbished, System Liquidation (need volume)

3. Manufacturer Partner Programs

Direct manufacturer partnerships provide better margins, co-marketing opportunities, and access to deal registration that protects your pricing.

System Manufacturers

Lenovo Partner Program

Best For: Business laptops (ThinkPad), low barrier to entry

Tier Structure:
  • Authorized: $0-$249K annual revenue - Entry level
  • Silver: $250K-$999K - Enhanced benefits
  • Gold: $1M-$9.9M - Premium support
  • Platinum: $10M+ - Top tier benefits
Key Benefits:
  • Deal registration for pricing protection
  • Lenovo Pro pricing plus 1% volume rebates
  • Up to 60 days interest-free credit (DLL partnership)
  • Tech specialist support for volume orders
  • Arena training program with points for courses

Partner Hub: lenovopartnerhub.com

Registration: partnerwithlenovo.com

Financing Partners: D&H, Ingram Micro, Synnex, Tech Data

Dell Technologies Partner Program

Best For: Business systems, servers, refurbished outlet

Program Tiers: Authorized → Gold → Platinum → Titanium

Requirements:
  • Sponsoring distributor reference (D&H, Ingram, etc.)
  • Tax ID/VAT and proof of business registration
  • Corporate URL with matching email domain
  • Reseller certificate or license (if applicable)
Key Benefits:
  • Access to Dell Partner Portal with tools and resources
  • Training and certification programs
  • Configuration, quoting, and purchasing tools
  • Dell Outlet Authorized Reseller Program for refurbished

Registration Guide: Dell Partner Registration

Support: Partner Support Portal

Outlet Program: Dell Outlet Reseller

HP Partner Program

Best For: Printers, consumer PCs, business systems

Two Paths to Reseller Status:
  1. Direct: Register at HP Partner Portal, sign HP Partner Agreement

Requirements:

Refurbished Supplier Selection Criteria

  • Verify NIST 800-88 data wiping certification
  • Look for 3+ years business history
  • Require minimum 12-month warranty
  • Check cosmetic grading systems (A/B/C)
  • Expect 30-50% savings vs. new devices at volume

8. Implementation Timeline

Week 1: Foundation

Prepare documentation, apply to D&H and Ingram Micro

  • Gather all required documents (tax ID, reseller cert, W-9)
  • Submit D&H Distributing application
  • Submit Ingram Micro reseller application
  • Apply to iFixit Pro wholesale program

Week 2: Manufacturer Programs

Apply to key manufacturer partner programs

  • Submit Lenovo Partner Program application
  • Apply to ASUS Valued Partner Program
  • Complete HP Partner Portal registration
  • Follow up on distributor applications

Week 3-4: Secondary Applications

Expand network with additional suppliers

  • Apply to Dell Partner Program
  • Register for MSI Partner Program
  • Set up Newegg Business account
  • Contact Micro Center Business Sales

Month 2: Training & Onboarding

Complete training, establish pricing, test orders

  • Complete distributor onboarding and training
  • Register for manufacturer certifications
  • Place test orders to verify processes
  • Set up account portals and ordering systems

Month 3+: Optimization

Evaluate performance, expand partnerships

  • Review pricing and margins by supplier
  • Apply for Intel/AMD partner programs
  • Explore refurbished bulk suppliers (once volume supports)
  • Negotiate better terms based on order history

9. Partnership Application Checklist

Gather these documents before applying to any supplier:

Required Documents

  • Business License / Certificate of Formation
  • Kansas Sales Tax Certificate (Reseller Certificate)
  • Federal Tax ID (EIN)
  • W-9 Form (completed and signed)
  • Certificate of Insurance (General Liability)
  • Bank Account Information (for credit applications)
  • Trade References (2-3 other suppliers you work with)

Business Information Needed

  • Legal Business Name and DBA
  • Business Address (physical location, not PO Box)
  • Years in Business
  • Number of Employees
  • Annual Revenue (estimated)
  • Primary Business Type (retail, repair, MSP, etc.)
  • Website URL with matching email domain

For Manufacturer Programs

  • Existing distributor relationship (sponsor reference)
  • Estimated annual purchases by product category
  • Target market (consumer, SMB, enterprise)
  • Sales team size (if applicable)
  • Showroom/demo capability description
  • Technical certification plans

Pro Tip: Prepare a One-Pager

Create a single-page overview of The Computer Store that includes: company history, services offered, target market, current suppliers, and growth plans. This can be attached to any application to strengthen your case.
