Market Research & Positioning

Week 1-2

1 Analyze Local Competition

Understanding your competitive landscape is essential for positioning and pricing.

  • Big Box Retailers: Best Buy, Micro Center (Kansas City) - Limited customization, cookie-cutter builds
  • Online System Integrators: NZXT BLD, iBUYPOWER, CyberPowerPC - Good quality but no local support
  • Local Competitors: Search for custom PC builders in Topeka/Lawrence area
  • Your Advantage: Local presence, same-day support, personalized consultation, Cruz's expertise

2 Define Target Customer Segments

Focus marketing efforts on the most profitable and accessible customer segments.

  • Gamers (Primary): Ages 16-35, want high FPS, RGB aesthetics, VR capability
  • Content Creators: Video editors, streamers, graphic designers need rendering power
  • Remote Professionals: Work-from-home employees needing reliable workstations
  • Small Businesses: CAD workstations, medical imaging, specialized applications
  • Holton Expansion: Farmers and agricultural businesses needing robust systems

💡 Market Insight

According to industry research, 67% of custom PC buyers value local support and the ability to see/touch components before purchase. This is your key differentiator over online builders like NZXT and CyberPowerPC.

4 Develop Manufacturer Relationships

Direct relationships with manufacturers can yield additional benefits and margin.

  • AMD Partner Program: Access to training, marketing materials, occasional rebates
  • Intel Partner Alliance: Similar benefits plus co-op marketing funds
  • NVIDIA Partner Network: GPU allocation priority during shortages
  • Corsair/NZXT/MSI: Builder programs with discounted samples for display

⚠️ Important: Managing GPU Allocation

GPU supply can be volatile. Establish relationships with multiple distributors and sign up for stock alerts. Consider keeping 1-2 popular GPUs in inventory for immediate builds. Never promise specific GPUs until stock is confirmed.

8 Implement Quality Assurance Testing

Every build must pass these tests before customer handoff:

  • Visual Inspection: Cable management, component seating, thermal paste application
  • POST Test: System boots to BIOS, all components detected
  • Memory Test: MemTest86 for minimum 2 passes (or overnight for premium)
  • Storage Test: CrystalDiskMark to verify SSD speeds match specs
  • CPU Stress: Prime95 or Cinebench R23 for 30+ minutes, monitor temps
  • GPU Stress: 3DMark Time Spy or Furmark for 30+ minutes
  • Thermal Verification: CPU under 85°C, GPU under 83°C under load
  • Full System: OCCT or AIDA64 combined stress for 1+ hour
  • Windows Updates: Fully updated, all drivers current
  • Documentation: Record test results, temps, and benchmark scores

Pre-Delivery Checklist

All stress tests passed (screenshots saved)
Windows activated and fully updated
All drivers installed (chipset, GPU, audio, LAN)
XMP/DOCP enabled for RAM
Professional photos taken (at least 5 angles)
Build documentation folder created (specs, benchmarks, receipts)
Dust filters and glass cleaned
Extra cables/accessories bagged with manuals

Marketing & Customer Acquisition

Week 6-8 & Ongoing

10 Create Showcase Content

Cruz's photography skills are a major asset. Every build is marketing material.

  • Photo Standards: Minimum 5 professional shots per build (hero, interior, RGB on, detail, with customer)
  • Website Gallery: Dedicated "Our Builds" page with specs, photos, testimonials
  • Social Media: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook posts for each completed build
  • Before/After: Show component boxes → finished build transformation
  • Timelapse Videos: Record builds for YouTube/TikTok (huge engagement)

11 Local Marketing Strategies

  • Display Builds: Keep 1-2 show builds in-store for customers to see/touch
  • Gaming Events: Sponsor local esports teams, LAN parties, gaming cafes
  • School Partnerships: Reach out to Washburn Tech, local high schools for student builds
  • Business Cards: Leave at gaming stores, campus bulletin boards
  • Google Business: Post weekly photos, get reviews from happy customers
  • Holton Expansion: Market to rural customers who lack local options

12 Online Presence & SEO

  • Target Keywords: "custom gaming PC Topeka", "PC builder Kansas", "custom computer build near me"
  • PCPartPicker Presence: Create builds, link to store
  • Reddit/Forums: Engage in r/buildapc, r/pcmasterrace with helpful advice
  • YouTube: Build videos, component reviews, local market positioning
  • Referral Program: $50 off next service for customer referrals

💡 Pro Tip: The Showroom Effect

A running RGB gaming PC in-store is your best salesperson. Customers who see and hear a quality build running games are 3x more likely to commission their own. Invest in one premium display build that showcases your best work.
