Pop-Up Repair Kiosk Partnership Opportunities
The Computer Store — Topeka, KS & Surrounding Communities
Compiled: March 11, 2026

Executive Summary

This report identifies 100+ potential partner venues across Topeka and 12 surrounding communities within a 45-minute drive radius. Partners are organized by phase (matching the rollout plan) and categorized by venue type. Phase 1 towns received the deepest research as immediate targets.

The strongest partnership opportunities fall into three tiers:

  • Highest Value: Senior centers and libraries — built-in audience of people who need tech help, community trust, existing programming infrastructure
  • High Value: Community centers, American Legion/VFW halls, and coworking spaces — foot traffic, community orientation, available space
  • Supporting Value: Coffee shops, insurance offices, hardware stores, and churches — ambient foot traffic, local business relationships, cross-promotion

Top 10 First Contacts (Highest ROI)

  1. Jayhawk Area Agency on Aging (Susan Harris) — Single relationship opens doors to all Shawnee County senior centers. Already involved in digital equity work.
  2. NEKAAA — Same for Jackson and Jefferson County towns (Holton, Perry, Oskaloosa).
  3. East Topeka Senior Center (Kelli Hough) — Largest, most active Topeka senior center. 150+ food box distribution = built-in monthly audience.
  4. Jackson County Senior Citizens Services (Holton) — Primary Phase 1 senior venue. Daily meals bring consistent foot traffic.
  5. Beck-Bookman Library, Holton (Amy Austin) — Trusted community institution in your #1 Phase 1 town.
  6. Oskaloosa Senior Citizens Center — County seat location, dedicated senior facility with built-in audience.
  7. Topeka LULAC Senior Center — Already offers computer classes. Natural extension to offer repair services.
  8. Omni Circle (Michael Odupitan) — Mission-aligned coworking. Entrepreneur and minority community connections.
  9. Topeka & Shawnee County Public Library — Already runs "Level Up" digital literacy across community centers. Potential system-wide partnership.
  10. Osage County Senior Center (Tammy Fager) — One relationship covers 5 Phase 3 towns.

Approach Strategy & Sample Pitches

Lead with the aging agencies (Jayhawk AAA, NEKAAA). They have the relationships, trust, and infrastructure to introduce you to every senior center in their service area. Libraries are second tier — they're already trusted tech-help destinations. Coffee shops and businesses are cross-promotion plays.

For Senior Centers

"We bring free tech help and affordable computer repair directly to your center on a regular schedule, so your members don't have to drive to Topeka."

For Libraries

"We partner with your library to offer monthly computer repair clinics — your patrons get trusted tech help, and the library becomes an even stronger community resource."

For Small Businesses

"We set up a small repair station at your location one day a month. Your customers see a convenient service, and we bring extra foot traffic to your business."

For Veterans Organizations

"Many veterans need help with VA telehealth, MyHealtheVet portal, and benefits claims. We bring a tech expert to your post so members can get their devices working and stay connected to their VA care."
