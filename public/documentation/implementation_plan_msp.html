What is MSP & Why It Matters

MSP (Managed Service Provider) means providing ongoing, proactive IT management for businesses - not just fixing things when they break. You become their outsourced IT department.

Market Opportunity: The global MSP market is projected to grow from $243B (2021) to $557B by 2028 - a 12.6% CAGR. Small businesses increasingly need IT support but can't afford full-time IT staff.

MSP vs. Your Current Business Model

Aspect Break-Fix (Current) MSP (New)
Revenue Model One-time per repair Monthly recurring (MRR)
Customer Relationship Transactional Ongoing partnership
Approach Reactive (fix when broken) Proactive (prevent problems)
Predictability Variable income Predictable monthly income
Client Lifetime Value $200-500 per visit $15,000-50,000+ over 3-5 years

Phase 1: Define Your Services

1

Define Your MSP Service Offerings

Week 1-2

Before you can sell MSP services, you need to clearly define what you're offering. Start simple and expand over time.

Core Services to Include:

  • Remote Monitoring & Management (RMM) - 24/7 monitoring of all client computers for issues, performance, and security
  • Patch Management - Automatic Windows updates, driver updates, and software patches
  • Antivirus/Endpoint Protection - Managed antivirus across all devices (you already sell this!)
  • Remote Support - Help desk support via phone, email, or remote access
  • Backup Verification - Ensure client backups are running and recoverable
  • Onsite Support - Scheduled visits for hands-on maintenance
  • Vendor Management - Handle calls with ISPs, software vendors, printers, etc.

Action Items:

  • Write a 1-page service description for each core offering
  • Define what's included vs. what's extra (scope of work)
  • Create a simple Service Level Agreement (SLA) template
  • Define response time guarantees (e.g., 4-hour response for critical issues)
2

Create Service Tiers

Week 2

Offer 2-3 service levels so clients can choose based on their needs and budget.

Essential

$75-100/user/month

  • RMM monitoring
  • Patch management
  • Managed antivirus
  • Email support
  • 8x5 support hours

Professional

$125-150/user/month

  • Everything in Essential
  • Phone + remote support
  • Quarterly onsite visits
  • Backup monitoring
  • Vendor management

Premium

$175-200/user/month

  • Everything in Professional
  • Monthly onsite visits
  • Advanced security (MFA, etc.)
  • Priority response (2-hour)
  • Quarterly business reviews

Pricing Note:

These are starting points. Research shows small businesses pay $100-250/user/month for comprehensive IT support. Start at the lower end for Topeka/Holton market, then raise prices for new clients as you gain experience.

3

Legal & Business Setup

Week 2-3
  • MSP Contract Template - Get a lawyer to review or use a template from ConnectWise/industry resources
  • Service Level Agreement (SLA) - Define response times, uptime guarantees, exclusions
  • Liability Insurance - Check if your current business insurance covers MSP services; may need E&O (Errors & Omissions) coverage
  • Business License - Verify your Topeka business license covers IT services
  • Terms of Service - What happens if client doesn't pay, scope creep, termination clauses

Key Contract Elements:

  • Minimum contract term (suggest 12 months with 30-day out clause)
  • Payment terms (net 15 or auto-pay required)
  • Scope of work - what's included, what's billable extra
  • Liability limitations
  • Termination and offboarding process

Start with just an RMM platform (Syncro). Add other tools as you grow and identify specific needs. Many MSPs waste money on tools they don't fully use.

6

Configure Your RMM

Week 5-6
  • Set up monitoring policies - What alerts do you want? (disk space, CPU, memory, offline devices)
  • Create patch management policies - When should updates install? (after hours recommended)
  • Build automation scripts - Common tasks like clearing temp files, restarting services
  • Set up ticketing workflow - How do tickets get created, assigned, escalated?
  • Create client onboarding checklist - Standard steps for deploying RMM agent to new client
  • Test on your own computers first! - Deploy to Computer Store machines before any clients

Phase 3: Pricing & Packaging

7

Finalize Your Pricing Model

Week 6-7

Choose Your Pricing Model:

Model How It Works Pros Cons
Per-User $X/month per employee Simple to explain; covers all devices per person Users with many devices cost more to support
Per-Device $X/month per computer/device Direct correlation to work; easy to track Tracking devices can be tedious
Tiered/Flat $X/month for up to Y users Predictable for client; simple to sell Risk of under/over-pricing

Recommendation: Per-User Pricing

Per-user is becoming the industry standard (21% of MSPs use it). It's simple for clients to understand and scales naturally.

  • Covers all devices the user has (desktop, laptop, phone)
  • Easy to quote: "How many employees do you have?"
  • Automatically adjusts as they hire/fire

Business Size Essential Professional Premium

10 users $750/month $1,250/month $1,750/month

Phase 4: Launch & Acquire First Clients

9

Pilot Program with Existing Customers

Week 8-10

The easiest first MSP clients are businesses you already serve. They know and trust you.

Identify Pilot Candidates:

  • Look in RepairShopr for business accounts that have visited 3+ times
  • Businesses you've done multiple repairs for
  • Any business owner who has said "I wish you could just handle all our IT"
  • Businesses Max has personal relationships with

Pilot Offer:

"We're launching managed IT services and looking for 3 pilot clients. In exchange for being our first and giving us feedback, we're offering:

  • First month FREE
  • Locked-in pricing for 2 years
  • Direct access to owner (Max) for any issues

Limit to 3-5 pilot clients to ensure quality service as you learn.

Pilot Client Onboarding Process:

  1. Discovery meeting (1-2 hours) - Understand their environment, pain points, current IT spend
  2. Network assessment - Document all computers, printers, network equipment
  3. Deploy RMM agent - Install on all computers
  4. Set up monitoring - Configure alerts specific to their environment
  5. Documentation - Record passwords, vendors, key info
  6. Training call - Show them how to submit tickets, what to expect

Networking

Sales Tip:

The best sales pitch is: "How much did your last IT problem cost you in downtime?" Then show how monthly proactive support prevents those costly surprises.

12

Expand Services Over Time

Month 6+

Add-On Services to Introduce Later:

  • Managed Backup - Cloud backup with verification ($50-100/server/month)
  • Email Security - Spam filtering, email protection ($3-5/user/month)
  • Cybersecurity Training - Phishing simulations for staff ($2-3/user/month)
  • Compliance Support - HIPAA documentation for medical clients
  • VoIP Phone Systems - Partner with a VoIP provider for referrals
  • Hardware as a Service - Lease computers to clients monthly

Scaling Milestones:

  • 5 clients: Evaluate if you need to hire help desk support
  • 10 clients: Consider dedicated MSP technician role
  • $10K MRR: Your MSP business is sustainable
  • $25K MRR: MSP may become primary business focus
