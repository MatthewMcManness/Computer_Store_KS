# Protection Plan Tiers Proposal

## Executive Summary

This proposal introduces Bronze and Gold tiers alongside the existing Silver Plan to capture a wider customer base while increasing average revenue per customer.

## Market Research Summary

**Industry Benchmarks:**
- Bask: Silver $9.99, Gold $19.99, Diamond $24.99, Platinum $34.99/month
- Heartland Computer: Gold Plan $299/year (~$24.92/month) for 3 devices
- GEEK-AID: $39.95-89.95/month for managed IT services
- Average tech support: $75-250/user/month for MSP services

**Current Computer Store KS Silver Plan: $24.99/month**
- Positioned competitively in the local market
- Includes comprehensive benefits that justify the price

## Proposed Tier Structure

### Bronze Plan - $14.99/month (3-month minimum)
**Target Customer:** Budget-conscious users, seniors on fixed income, light computer users

**Features:**
- Antivirus software included
- 25% discount on virus removal services
- 10% discount on labor
- Free in-store diagnostics and estimates
- Basic email support (24-48hr response)
- Quarterly system health check

**Business Value:**
- Captures price-sensitive customers who wouldn't sign up otherwise
- Lower discounts = higher margin on paid services
- Entry point to upsell to Silver
- Estimated margin: 65-70%

**Customer Value:**
- $180/year savings on antivirus alone
- Entry-level protection without big commitment
- Still gets core discounts on services

---

### Silver Plan - $24.99/month (3-month minimum) [CURRENT]
**Target Customer:** Average home users, families, remote workers

**Features:**
- All Bronze features PLUS:
- Antivirus software included
- 50% discount on virus removal services
- Half-price house calls
- 50% off account recovery assistance
- Preventive maintenance service
- Limited help desk/remote support (2hrs/month)
- Performance monitoring and alerts
- 15% discount on labor
- Priority scheduling (same-day when available)

**Business Value:**
- Proven tier with existing customer base
- Good balance of value and profitability
- Estimated margin: 55-60%

**Customer Value:**
- Comprehensive protection for typical user
- Significant savings on common services
- Peace of mind with monitoring

---

### Gold Plan - $39.99/month (3-month minimum)
**Target Customer:** Power users, home office/remote workers, small business owners, tech-dependent users

**Features:**
- All Silver features PLUS:
- Premium antivirus + malware protection suite
- 75% discount on virus removal services
- FREE house calls (up to 2/month)
- FREE account recovery assistance
- Priority preventive maintenance (monthly)
- Extended remote support (4hrs/month)
- 25% discount on labor
- Annual comprehensive tune-up included
- Data backup consultation and setup
- VIP priority scheduling (guaranteed same-day)
- Dedicated support line
- 10% discount on hardware purchases

**Business Value:**
- Higher recurring revenue per customer
- Attracts high-value customers who use computers for work
- Strong customer lock-in and loyalty
- House calls included but limited = predictable costs
- Estimated margin: 50-55%

**Customer Value:**
- Premium service for those who depend on computers
- Free house calls alone worth $150+/year
- Best discounts on all services
- White-glove treatment

---

## Pricing Psychology & Strategy

### Good-Better-Best Model
- **Bronze** = Anchor (makes Silver look like great value)
- **Silver** = Target (most customers should land here)
- **Gold** = Aspirational (captures high-value customers)

### Price Gaps
- Bronze to Silver: +$10/month (+67%) for significantly more value
- Silver to Gold: +$15/month (+60%) for premium perks

### Minimum Commitment
- 3-month minimum on all tiers prevents churn abuse
- Consider: 6-month minimum for Gold with first month free promo

## Implementation Costs

### One-Time Setup
- RepairShopr custom field updates: 1 hour
- Website updates: 4-6 hours
- Marketing materials: 2-3 hours
- Staff training: 1 hour
- **Total: ~10 hours of work**

### Ongoing Costs
- Antivirus licenses: ~$3-5/user/month (bulk pricing)
- Remote support tools: Already have
- House calls (Gold): ~2-3 additional/month estimated

---

## Migration Strategy

### Existing Silver Customers
- Grandfathered at current rate for 6 months
- Option to upgrade to Gold with first month free
- No forced changes

### New Customer Acquisition
- Promote all three tiers on website
- Default recommendation: Silver (best value)
- Bronze for price objections
- Gold for business/power users

---

## Risk Analysis

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Bronze cannibalizes Silver | Price gap makes Silver clearly better value |
| Gold house calls exceed budget | Cap at 2/month, additional at 50% off |
| Churn at minimum commitment | 3-month minimum, consider loyalty bonuses |
| Antivirus costs eat margins | Negotiate bulk licensing, use quality free tier for Bronze |

---

## Recommendation

**Implement all three tiers with:**
1. Bronze at $14.99/month - capture budget customers
2. Silver at $24.99/month - maintain proven middle tier
3. Gold at $39.99/month - premium tier for power users

**Expected Outcome:**
- 60%+ increase in protection plan subscribers within 12 months
- 40%+ increase in recurring revenue
- Higher customer satisfaction through choice
- Competitive differentiation in Topeka market
