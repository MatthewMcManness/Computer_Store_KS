Executive Summary

This document provides a comprehensive comparison of RMM (Remote Monitoring & Management) platforms to help Computer Store KS decide whether to continue with NinjaOne (currently used for consumer protection plans) or switch to an alternative as you expand into MSP services.

We evaluate five options: NinjaOne (current), Syncro, ConnectWise, Datto RMM, and building your own solution with open-source tools like Tactical RMM.

Bottom Line Recommendation: Stay with NinjaOne

For your situation, staying with NinjaOne is the right choice. You already have it deployed for consumer plans, it integrates directly with RepairShopr (your existing PSA), and expanding it for business MSP clients requires zero migration. The per-device pricing works in your favor at small scale, and NinjaOne's patch management is industry-leading. Switching platforms would cost you time, money, and introduce risk for minimal benefit.

Exception: If you grow past 200+ managed devices AND want to consolidate to a single platform (eliminating RepairShopr), then Syncro becomes worth reconsidering.

Key Consideration: You're Already Invested

You've already deployed NinjaOne agents to consumer devices. Switching platforms means:

  • Uninstalling NinjaOne agents from existing devices
  • Installing new RMM agents on all devices
  • Recreating all your policies, scripts, and automations
  • Learning a new platform and retraining your team
  • Potential disruption to existing customers

This switching cost is real and significant. Only switch if the benefits dramatically outweigh these costs.

Hidden Costs of Switching

The numbers above don't include switching costs. If you leave NinjaOne, factor in:

  • Migration time: 20-40 hours to reinstall agents, recreate policies, and test
  • Learning curve: 10-20 hours per technician to learn new platform
  • Disruption risk: Potential for monitoring gaps during transition
  • RepairShopr integration: If switching to Syncro, you lose RepairShopr OR pay for both

At $50/hour labor cost, migration alone could cost $1,000-$2,000+

The Real Math: NinjaOne Makes Sense

Yes, Syncro is ~$1,500/year cheaper at 100 endpoints. But:

  • You already have NinjaOne deployed and working
  • Native RepairShopr integration saves you time every day
  • Switching costs eat the first year's "savings"
  • NinjaOne's patch management and support are superior

The break-even point for switching is ~18-24 months, assuming no hiccups.

Decision Scenarios: When to Stay vs. Switch

Scenario A: You're Happy with RepairShopr

You like RepairShopr for ticketing and invoicing. It works well for your retail repair business and you don't want to change.

STAY with NinjaOne

NinjaOne has native RepairShopr integration. No other RMM integrates as well (Syncro would replace it).

Scenario B: You Want One Platform

Managing two platforms (RMM + PSA) feels like overhead. You want everything in one place and would consider ditching RepairShopr.

CONSIDER Syncro

Syncro is a true all-in-one. But only switch when you're ready to fully commit and migrate everything.

Scenario C: You Have 10-50 Devices

You're just starting MSP with a handful of endpoints from 2-3 pilot customers.

STAY with NinjaOne

At low volume, per-device pricing isn't painful. Focus on winning clients, not switching platforms.

Scenario D: You're at 200+ Devices

Your MSP has grown significantly and per-device costs are mounting.

RE-EVALUATE Options

At scale, per-technician pricing (Syncro) becomes more economical. But negotiate volume discounts with NinjaOne first.

Scenario E: You're Technical & Have Time

Matthew or another team member loves tinkering with servers, has Linux experience, and enjoys self-hosting.

CONSIDER Tactical RMM (for fun/testing)

Set up Tactical RMM on a spare VPS as a learning project. But don't bet your business on it yet.

Scenario F: Enterprise Clients Come Knocking

You land a 100+ seat client with complex compliance requirements (HIPAA, etc.).

CONSIDER ConnectWise or Datto

Enterprise clients may require enterprise-grade tools. But this is a champagne problem you don't have yet.

RepairShopr Integration: Why It Matters

Since you already use RepairShopr, the integration story is critical:

NinjaOne + RepairShopr Integration Features

  • Automatic device sync: Devices in NinjaOne automatically appear in RepairShopr as assets
  • Alert-to-ticket conversion: NinjaOne alerts can create RepairShopr tickets automatically
  • Customer matching: Devices are linked to the correct RepairShopr customer
  • Two-way data sync: Changes in either platform stay synchronized
  • No Zapier needed: Native integration, not a third-party connector

What You'd Lose by Switching to Syncro

Syncro is an all-in-one platform that REPLACES RepairShopr. If you switch:

  • You'd need to migrate ALL customer data from RepairShopr to Syncro
  • Your retail repair workflow would change (new ticketing system)
  • Invoicing, inventory, and reporting would all be different
  • Training for your team on the new PSA

This is a much bigger change than just switching RMM.

Syncro + RepairShopr via Zapier?

Technically you could run both and connect them via Zapier, but:

  • You'd be paying for two PSA platforms (wasteful)
  • Zapier adds latency and potential failure points
  • No native integration means manual workarounds

If you switch to Syncro, you should fully commit and leave RepairShopr.

Should You Build Your Own RMM?

Building your own solution with open-source tools like Tactical RMM is tempting. Here's the reality:

When DIY Makes Sense

  • You have strong Linux/DevOps skills on your team
  • You manage 500+ endpoints (licensing savings become significant)
  • You have specific customization needs commercial tools can't meet
  • You enjoy the technical challenge and learning
  • You have time to maintain and troubleshoot the system
  • Data sovereignty is critical (healthcare, government)

When DIY Doesn't Make Sense

  • You're just starting your MSP (focus on sales, not servers)
  • Your team is already stretched thin
  • You need guaranteed uptime and SLAs for clients
  • You don't have Linux/Docker expertise in-house
  • You need 24/7 support when things break
  • Time spent on infrastructure = time not spent on clients

Tactical RMM: The Leading Open Source Option

If you ever do go DIY, Tactical RMM is the best option for MSPs:

  • Purpose-built for MSPs (not repurposed monitoring tool)
  • Web-based dashboard with multi-tenant support
  • Scripting, automation, and patch management included
  • MeshCentral integration for remote access
  • Active community with regular updates

Server requirements: VPS with 4GB+ RAM, Debian 12 recommended, 3 subdomains (rmm, api, mesh)

Setup time: 4-8 hours for initial deployment, days to configure properly
