About the Business

Business Type:

Local computer repair shop serving the Topeka, Kansas area with computer repair services, refurbished computer sales, and technology solutions.

Services Offered:

  • Computer Repair: Diagnostics, virus removal, hardware upgrades
  • Data Services: Data transfer, backup, and recovery
  • OS Installation: Windows, Linux, macOS installation and configuration
  • Custom Computers: Custom-built PCs tailored to customer needs
  • Refurbished Sales: Quality refurbished desktops and laptops
  • Debloat Services: Windows optimization and bloatware removal
  • Antivirus Solutions: Security software installation and support

Target Market:

  • Local Topeka residents and businesses
  • Budget-conscious consumers seeking quality refurbished computers
  • Users needing reliable computer repair services
  • Linux enthusiasts and those interested in alternative operating systems
