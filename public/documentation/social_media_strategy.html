Executive Summary

The Computer Store has a minimal but established social media presence. Currently active on Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), and Nextdoor, but with low engagement and inconsistent posting. Major opportunities exist on Google Business Profile optimization, YouTube, LinkedIn (for B2B MSP services), and TikTok. Given the 2026 growth plans including MSP expansion and Holton location, a robust social media strategy will be essential for brand awareness and lead generation.

Social Media Expansion Strategy

Strategic Focus: Prioritize platforms that drive local customer discovery (Google, Facebook, Nextdoor) and B2B lead generation (LinkedIn). Build YouTube presence for long-term SEO and authority. Use Instagram and TikTok for engagement and brand awareness with younger demographics.

Platform Prioritization Matrix

Tier Platforms Audience Primary Goal
Tier 1 - Critical Google Business, Facebook, LinkedIn Local consumers, B2B clients Lead generation, local SEO, MSP credibility
Tier 2 - Growth YouTube, Instagram Broad audience, DIYers, enthusiasts Authority building, engagement, brand awareness
Tier 3 - Experimental TikTok, X (Twitter) Younger demo, tech community Viral potential, brand personality
Tier 4 - Maintain Nextdoor, Yelp Local neighbors Reputation management, local presence

Content Pillar Strategy

Educational (40%)

Tech tips, how-tos, security advice, maintenance guides. Positions The Computer Store as the trusted local expert.

Behind-the-Scenes (25%)

Repair process videos, custom build showcases, team introductions, shop tours. Builds trust and humanizes the brand.

Community (20%)

Customer success stories, local events, Topeka/Holton news, Right to Repair advocacy. Connects with community.

Promotional (15%)

Sales, protection plans, new inventory, holiday specials. Drive revenue but keep minimal to avoid being "salesy."

Content Plan by Platform

Facebook Content Strategy

Posting Frequency: 4-5 posts per week

  • Monday: Tech Tip Monday - Quick computer maintenance or security tip
  • Wednesday: Workshop Wednesday - Behind-the-scenes repair or custom build progress
  • Friday: Feature Friday - Showcase refurbished computers, customer stories, or team member spotlights
  • Weekend: Engagement posts - Questions, polls, local community content

Instagram Content Strategy

Posting Frequency: 3-4 feed posts per week + daily stories

  • Feed: High-quality photos of repairs, custom builds, before/after transformations
  • Reels: 2x per week - Quick repair clips, tech tips, trending audio mashups
  • Stories: Daily - Shop life, team moments, quick polls, customer shoutouts
  • Hashtags: #TopekaTech #KansasSmallBusiness #ComputerRepair #PCBuilds #RightToRepair

YouTube Content Strategy (New)

Posting Frequency: 1-2 videos per week (start with 2 per month)

  • Tutorial Series: "5 Minute Fixes" - Quick tips homeowners can do
  • Build Logs: Custom PC build process from parts to finished product
  • Tech Reviews: Budget laptops, refurbished computers, peripherals
  • Right to Repair: Advocacy content, repair vs replace economics
  • Shop Tours: Meet the team, see the workshop

LinkedIn Content Strategy (New - B2B Focus)

Posting Frequency: 3 posts per week

  • Cybersecurity Updates: Threats affecting Topeka businesses
  • MSP Benefits: Cost savings, productivity, peace of mind
  • Case Studies: How we helped local businesses
  • Industry Insights: IT trends relevant to small business
  • Networking: Engage with Greater Topeka Partnership, Chamber members

Google Business Posts

Posting Frequency: 2-3 updates per week

  • Offers: Current promotions and specials
  • What's New: New services, inventory arrivals
  • Events: Workshops, community involvement
  • Photos: Regular updates of work, team, shop

Implementation Timeline

Week 1-2: Foundation

  • Audit and optimize existing Facebook page (complete profile, add CTA button)
  • Claim/verify Google Business Profile, add all services and photos
  • Create LinkedIn company page for "The Computer Store / Network Technologies"
  • Set up social media management tool (Buffer, Hootsuite, or Later)
  • Create branded graphics templates in Canva

Week 3-4: Content Production

  • Photograph shop, team, workbench, and recent projects
  • Record first 2-3 YouTube videos (can be simple smartphone videos)
  • Create content calendar for first month
  • Set up Instagram Reels workflow
  • Write LinkedIn posts introducing the business to B2B audience

Month 2: Launch & Consistency

  • Launch YouTube channel with 2-3 initial videos
  • Begin consistent posting schedule across all platforms
  • Respond to all reviews and messages within 4 hours
  • Cross-promote content across platforms
  • Start engaging with local Topeka business accounts

Month 3: Optimization

  • Analyze metrics and adjust strategy
  • A/B test content types and posting times
  • Consider TikTok launch if Instagram Reels performing well
  • Begin customer review request program
  • Explore paid promotion for top-performing content

Month 4-6: Scale & Refine

  • Increase YouTube content to weekly
  • Launch customer referral social campaign
  • Prepare Holton expansion social strategy
  • Create location-specific content for new market
  • Build influencer/partnership relationships with local content creators

Tools & Resources Needed

Social Media Management

  • Buffer or Later: Schedule posts, manage multiple platforms (~$15/month)
  • Canva Pro: Branded graphics and video editing (~$13/month)
  • Google Analytics: Track social traffic (Free)

Content Creation

  • Smartphone: Modern smartphone camera is sufficient for most content
  • Ring Light: Better lighting for videos (~$25)
  • Tripod/Phone Mount: Stable shots (~$30)
  • Basic Microphone: Better audio for YouTube (~$50)

Time Investment

  • Content Creation: 3-4 hours/week
  • Engagement & Replies: 30 min/day
  • Analytics Review: 1 hour/week
  • Total: ~7-10 hours/week
