The computer repair industry is undergoing significant transformation. While traditional repair services face pricing pressure from cheap replacement electronics, several high-margin segments are experiencing strong growth:

📈 Growing Segments

  • Hardware Repair Services: $6.39B market, growing 6.5% CAGR to $10.65B by 2033

Growth Strategies

These strategies are prioritized by revenue potential, implementation complexity, and alignment with your team's existing capabilities:

1 Managed IT Services (MSP) for Small Businesses High Priority

MSP = Managed Service Provider - This is proactive IT management for businesses, NOT the same as your consumer Bronze/Silver/Gold plans.

How MSP Differs from Your Current Plans:
Your Bronze/Silver/Gold plans are great for individual consumers - antivirus, repair discounts, priority scheduling.

MSP contracts are for businesses (5-50 employees) - you manage ALL their computers, provide help desk support for their staff, monitor their network, verify backups, and prevent problems before they happen.

$3,000 - $15,000+/month potential recurring revenue

MSP vs. Consumer Plans Comparison:

Feature Bronze/Silver/Gold MSP Business Contract
Target Customer Individual/Family Small Business (5-50 employees)
Pricing Model $15-25/month per device $50-150/month per user
Support Model Reactive (fix when broken) Proactive (monitor & prevent)
Scope Single device Entire business IT infrastructure
Includes Antivirus, discounts, priority Help desk, monitoring, backups, network, security

Implementation Steps:

  • Define business service tiers: Basic (monitoring + remote support), Standard (+ onsite visits), Premium (+ cybersecurity)
  • Target: Dental offices, law firms, accounting firms, small manufacturers in Topeka AND Holton
  • Package: Monthly retainer covering unlimited remote support, quarterly onsite visits, backup verification
  • Tools: Implement RMM software (NinjaRMM, Syncro, or ConnectWise), use existing RepairShopr for ticketing
  • Marketing: Create dedicated MSP landing page, leverage Max's local business contacts
Quick Win: Start with 3-5 existing business customers. Offer first month free as pilot program. Average MSP client lifetime value is 3-5 years.

4 Linux Migration Services (Post-Windows 10 EOL) Medium Priority

Why: Windows 10 support ended October 14, 2025. The initial rush is over, but there's now a steady stream of users with unsupported systems seeking alternatives. Over 780,000 users switched to Linux during the EOL transition. Linux desktop share has hit 5% in the US. You already have a "Why Linux" page - continue building this niche.

Current Landscape: Many users are now running unsupported Windows 10, creating ongoing security risks. Position Linux as the solution for older hardware that can't run Windows 11.

$75-$150 per migration + refurbished computer sales synergy

Service Packages:

  • Linux Refresh: Install Linux on customer's existing PC ($75-$100)
  • Linux Ready PC: Refurbished computer with Linux pre-installed (built-in margin)
  • Linux Migration Plus: Installation + data transfer + 1-hour training ($150-$200)
  • Business Linux Transition: For offices wanting to escape Microsoft licensing

Target Customers:

  • Users still running unsupported Windows 10 (security risk messaging)
  • Budget-conscious consumers with older hardware that can't run Windows 11
  • Privacy-focused users
  • Schools and non-profits with tight budgets
  • Developers who prefer Linux environments

5 Smart Home Setup Services (Plug-and-Play Focus) Medium Priority

Why: $83.65B market growing at 13.4% CAGR. Many customers buy smart devices but struggle with setup, app configuration, and network integration. This is a service gap you can fill without licensing requirements.

Topeka Licensing Reality: Joseph has valuable electrical knowledge from his apprenticeship, but Topeka requires licensed journeyman/master electricians for electrical work (apprentices must work under 1:1 supervision). Security/alarm installation requires a separate Police Department license + ESA certification. Focus on services that DON'T require licensing.

$50-$150 per setup + device sales

What You CAN Offer (No License Required):

Service Price Range Notes
Smart speaker/display setup (Alexa, Google Home) $25-50 Plug-and-play, app config
Smart plug installation & automation $15-30 per device No wiring required
WiFi network optimization $75-150 Mesh setup, range extenders
Smart TV / streaming device setup $50-100 Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV
Whole-home smart integration $100-200 Connect all devices, routines
Smart thermostat swap (existing wires only) $50-75 Only if no C-wire work needed*

What Requires Licensing (Refer Out or License Up):

  • Hardwired smart switches/outlets: Requires Topeka journeyman/master electrical license
  • Thermostat C-wire installation: Requires electrical license
  • Security camera wiring: Requires alarm installer license (Police Dept + ESA cert)
  • Alarm systems: Requires $300 license + background check + 6-week processing
  • Doorbell camera hardwiring: Electrical license required

Options for Expanding Services:

Option A: Partner with Licensed Electrician

  • Refer hardwired jobs to a trusted local electrician
  • Negotiate referral fee (10-20%)
  • You handle device sales + software setup
  • They handle wiring work

Option B: Joseph Gets Licensed

  • 4,000 hours experience + 240 hours classroom
  • Pass 75%+ on NEC exam
  • $53 application fee (journeyman)
  • 6 hours continuing ed per year
  • Timeline: 6-12+ months if he has the hours
Joseph's Real Value: His electrical knowledge makes him excellent at hardware diagnostics, PSU troubleshooting, power-related repairs, and explaining technical concepts to customers. These skills don't require a license and are valuable differentiators for computer repair.

9 Retro Gaming Lounge (In-Store Attraction) Lower Priority

Why: The retro gaming market is $3.8B and growing 10% CAGR. This is a marketing investment, not a revenue center - designed to differentiate the store, increase foot traffic, and reinforce your gaming PC brand. Uses recycled hardware you already have.

Important: This is NOT recommended as a pay-per-hour arcade (overhead vs. revenue doesn't work). Instead, offer FREE play as a customer attraction and marketing tool.

Investment (One-Time):

Item Cost Notes
2-3 Bartop/Tabletop Cabinets $150-300 each DIY with Raspberry Pi + RetroPie
1 Full-Size Cabinet (Showpiece) $400-600 DIY build, Instagram-worthy
Recycled Monitors/Hardware $0 From existing inventory
Total Investment $850-1,500 One-time cost

Strategic Benefits:

  • Differentiation: No other Topeka repair shop has this - memorable experience
  • Dwell Time: "Play while you wait for your repair" - reduces perceived wait time
  • Content Marketing: Cruz photographs the setup for social media
  • Gaming PC Cross-Sell: "Like retro games? Wait until you see what a custom gaming PC can do"
  • Community Building: Host monthly retro gaming nights (free, builds loyalty)
  • Nostalgia Appeal: Attracts millennials and Gen X with disposable income - your custom PC buyers

Why NOT Pay-Per-Hour:

  • Insurance rider: $1,000-2,000/year additional
  • Amusement licensing required in most jurisdictions
  • At $10/hour × 50 hours/month = $500 gross, minus overhead = $200-300 net
  • Same time spent getting 1 MSP client = $500-1,500/month recurring
  • Standalone arcades require food/alcohol to be profitable

Implementation:

  • Build: Use Raspberry Pi 4 + RetroPie for each cabinet
  • Games: Classic arcade (Pac-Man, Galaga), NES, SNES, Genesis era
  • Space: 100-200 sq ft corner of store
  • Branding: Custom cabinet artwork featuring The Computer Store logo
Quick Win: Start with 1 bartop cabinet as a proof of concept. If customers love it, expand. If not, repurpose the hardware - minimal risk.
