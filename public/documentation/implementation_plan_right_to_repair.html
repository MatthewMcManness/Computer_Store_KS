🔧 Executive Summary

The Right to Repair movement is transforming consumer expectations. 87% of Americans support Right to Repair legislation, and states are passing laws requiring manufacturers to provide repair parts and documentation. By positioning Computer Store KS as the local champion of repair rights, you differentiate from disposable-culture competitors while building deep customer loyalty and recurring revenue.

This guide covers iFixit partnership programs, messaging strategy, parts sourcing, community building, and advocacy opportunities specific to Kansas.

iFixit Partnership Programs

Week 1-2

1 Understand iFixit Partnership Options

iFixit offers two distinct programs for repair businesses. Choose based on your repair volume and business model.

🛠️ iFixit Partnership Programs

iFixit Pro

Free to join

  • ✓ Wholesale pricing (15-40% off retail)
  • ✓ Access to professional-grade parts
  • ✓ Priority support from iFixit team
  • ✓ Marketing materials and co-branding
  • ✓ Access to repair guides and documentation
  • ✓ Listed in iFixit's repair shop directory
  • ✓ Free toolkits for qualifying shops

Best for: Computer repair shops doing regular iPhone, laptop, and device repairs

iFixit Reseller

Application required

  • ✓ Deeper wholesale discounts
  • ✓ Sell iFixit products to consumers
  • ✓ Complete toolkit inventory
  • ✓ Parts kits for common repairs
  • ✓ Retail display materials
  • ✓ Higher volume requirements

Best for: Shops wanting to sell iFixit toolkits and parts retail

2 Apply for iFixit Pro

The application process is straightforward. Here's what you need:

  • Business verification: EIN, business license, physical location
  • Repair experience: Description of your repair services
  • Website: Professional web presence (you have this!)
  • Apply at: pro.ifixit.com

✓ iFixit Pro Benefits for Computer Store KS

  • Wholesale pricing on iPhone/Samsung/laptop batteries, screens, and parts
  • Professional Mako Driver Kit and Pro Tech Toolkit at cost
  • Listed in iFixit's "Find a Repair Shop" directory for Kansas
  • Co-branded "iFixit Repair Partner" badge for your website and store
  • Access to comprehensive repair guides (even for devices you rarely see)

💡 DIY Repair Kits - Revenue Opportunity

Offer curated DIY repair kits for customers who want to fix their own devices. Bundle iFixit parts with printed guides and charge a small markup. This builds trust, differentiates you from "we won't help you" shops, and creates upsell opportunities when DIY customers realize they need professional help.

Example: iPhone Battery Kit ($35 parts + guide) → Customer attempts → Comes back for screen repair → Becomes loyal customer

Messaging & Brand Positioning

Week 3-5

4 Define Your Right to Repair Message

Craft messaging that resonates with Kansas values: independence, thrift, sustainability, and supporting local.

Message Pillar Key Points Example Copy
Choice & Independence • You own it, you should be able to fix it
• Don't be forced to buy new
• Take control of your devices 		"Your device, your choice. We believe if you buy it, you should be able to fix it — or have someone local do it for you."
Save Money • Repair costs fraction of replacement
• Extend device lifespan
• Kansas families work hard for their money 		"Why spend $1,000 on a new laptop when a $150 repair gives you 3 more years? We help Topeka families stretch their tech dollars."
Environmental • 50M tons of e-waste annually
• Repair reduces landfill
• Sustainability without sacrifice 		"Every repair keeps electronics out of landfills. When you choose repair, you're choosing a cleaner Kansas."
Support Local • Money stays in Topeka/Holton
• We're your neighbors
• Face-to-face service 		"Skip the Genius Bar. We're right here in Topeka, and we'll treat your devices like our own."
Right to Know • Transparency in pricing
• We explain every repair
• Education, not intimidation 		"We'll always tell you what's wrong, what it costs, and whether it's worth fixing. No secrets, no surprises."

5 Create Right to Repair Content

  • Website page: "Why We Support Right to Repair" — values statement + local positioning
  • Blog series: "DIY Repair Guides" for common issues (links to iFixit guides when appropriate)
  • Social media: Repair vs Replace cost comparisons, before/after photos
  • Signage: "Repair Champion" badge in-store, window decal
  • Email signature: "Proud supporter of the Right to Repair"

⚠️ Messaging Balance

Don't be preachy or political. Focus on practical benefits (saving money, convenience, local service) rather than abstract advocacy. Kansas customers respond to common-sense economics more than environmental messaging. Lead with savings, follow with sustainability.

Community Building & Events

Q2 2026 & Ongoing

8 Launch Community Initiatives

Build loyalty and awareness through community engagement around repair culture.

  • Repair Café: Monthly free diagnostic events (bring any device, we'll tell you what's wrong)
  • DIY Workshops: Teach basic repairs (phone screen, RAM upgrade, SSD installation)
  • School Partnerships: STEM presentations about electronics and repair careers
  • Library Events: Partner with Topeka/Holton libraries for tech help days
  • Senior Tech Help: Special hours for seniors to get devices explained

9 Create a Repair Loyalty Program

Reward customers who choose repair over replacement:

  • "Repair Champion" Card: Get stamped for each repair, 5th repair gets 20% off
  • Referral Bonus: Refer a friend, both get $15 off next repair
  • Trade-In Credit: Bring in old devices for recycling, get store credit
  • DIY Discount: Customers who try DIY first get 10% off professional repair

✓ Community Event ROI

Repair cafés and workshops cost little (staff time, basic supplies) but generate significant returns:

  • Free diagnostics convert to paid repairs ~40% of the time
  • Workshop attendees become long-term customers (they trust you)
  • Local media coverage worth thousands in advertising
  • Google reviews and word-of-mouth from grateful attendees
