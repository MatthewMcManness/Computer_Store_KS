Important: The Real Value is Beyond Fees

While the transaction fee difference is modest, Stripe's real value comes from: (1) Zero monthly fees saving $300+/year, (2) Built-in features like BNPL, invoicing, and subscriptions at no extra cost, (3) Modern API enabling website integration and automation, (4) Tablet-based POS eliminating expensive proprietary hardware, (5) Single dashboard for all payments across both locations.

TD Synnex: Branded Merchandise & Dropshipping

What is TD Synnex?

TD Synnex is the world's largest IT distributor with 1.9+ million SKUs from major brands like HP, Dell, Lenovo, Microsoft, and more. Their MyShop platform enables resellers to sell branded products with YOUR company name on every package - customers never know it came from a distributor.

📦 Blind Shipping

Every package arrives with YOUR branding - "The Computer Store" on labels, packing slips, and documentation. Zero TD Synnex branding visible to customers.

🏪 In-Store Ordering

Order products for in-store sale at wholesale prices. No minimum orders. Ship to your store or directly to customers.

🌐 Dropship E-Commerce

Sell products on your website without holding inventory. Customer orders → you order from TD Synnex → they ship branded to customer.

💰 Wholesale Pricing

Access to distributor pricing typically 15-40% below retail. Markup for profit while still being competitive.

🔌 API Integration

Digital Bridge Platform provides REST APIs for real-time pricing, inventory, and order automation. Can integrate with your website.

📊 No Inventory Risk

Dropship model means you don't tie up capital in inventory. Order only what you've already sold.
